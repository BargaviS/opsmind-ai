COMPANY IT AND SECURITY POLICY

Accounts and Passwords:
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.
- Never share your password with anyone, including the IT team.

Devices:
- Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
- Personal USB drives are not allowed on company computers.
- Software installation requires written approval from the IT department.

Data and Network:
- Company data must be backed up daily to the cloud.
- A VPN is required when working from public Wi-Fi.
- Visitors must wear a badge and be escorted at all times.

Support:
- The IT helpdesk is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays.
- Phishing emails should be forwarded to security@company.example.
